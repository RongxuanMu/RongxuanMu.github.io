VibeXR Gallery

Immersive canvases you can step into

VibeXR Gallery collects living WebXR reconstructions of familiar paintings. Each piece turns a historical canvas into a room-scale field of brushstrokes: you can stir the pigment in the browser, or enter immersive mode with a headset and move through the scene with 6DoF tracking and optional hand tracking.

These studies sit between spatial design, procedural graphics, and XR interaction. The goal is not a still reproduction, but a space you can inhabit — art that responds when you reach toward it.
